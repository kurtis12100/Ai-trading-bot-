# Molten-metal furnace and crucible lifetime — feasibility brief (2026-11-20)

**Scope:** screening known refractory/coating families for longer service in foundry induction furnaces and crucibles. This is NOT a new or validated compound, and is not a qualified blast-furnace lining specification.

## Distinction of equipment
- Ironmaking blast furnace hearth: continuous operation, large carbon-block / ceramic-cup refractory system with protective 'skull', controlled cooling, and long campaign intervals. Coating alone cannot substitute for hearth thermal engineering or prevent burn-through.
- Induction melting furnace: consumable refractory working lining, subject to molten metal and slag reaction, erosion, thermal cycling, and physical impacts; more tractable pilot use case.
- Removable crucible: different materials and failure modes for molten aluminum, iron, steel, or reactive metals; no universal coating.

## Baseline families / known prior art
1. **Alumina–magnesia spinel (Al2O3–MgAl2O4) / alumina-magnesia refractory.** Established steelmaking lining family. Target: slag infiltration, metal penetration, spalling and repeated-cycle survival. 2026 experimental paper: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0955221926002967
2. **Yttria (Y2O3)-based refractory barrier.** Known approaches to reactive molten-metal containment; high cost / reactivity dependent. Prior patent: https://patents.google.com/patent/US20010033950A1/en
3. **Boron nitride nonwetting coatings.** Established chiefly for molten-aluminum applications; insufficient basis for 1500 C iron/steel or universal blast furnace use. Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US11692101B2/en and https://patents.google.com/patent/US6051058A/en

## First design-of-experiment recommendation
**Application:** noncritical, replaceable foundry test crucibles or prepared test coupons for a chosen alloy and slag chemistry, screened under a qualified refractory laboratory's controlled conditions.

**Compare:** (A) incumbent commercial lining; (B) commercial alumina-spinel product; (C) commercial compatible repair/overlay product; (D) a later experimental design after literature/patent vetting. Three or more replicate samples per condition, with consistent substrate, surface area, thermal history and melt/slag chemistry.

**Measure:** metal/slag penetration depth (mm), recession per heat (mm/heat), thermal shock cracking/spall area, mass loss, material contamination of melt, usable heats until failure, installation/curing time and total cost per heat. Include microscopy + phase analysis; require a professional melt-handling laboratory. Never test improvised crucible/coating combinations in a working furnace.

**Decision gates:** demonstrate repeatable lower erosion or longer usable life *versus incumbent under matched conditions*; secure an industrial test partner; assess total cost per heat and repairability; do a competent novelty/FTO patent review before claiming invention or disclosing recipes.
