## Experimental hypothesis
Comparing alumina-rich versus near-stoichiometric spinel-forming compositions may illuminate tradeoffs between alumina/spinel phases and their response to steel slag. It does **not** establish the near-stoichiometric blend is superior. Particle-size distribution, aggregate, binders, curing, phase evolution, porosity, and slag chemistry matter more than this isolated balance. More spinel is not automatically better; spinel formation can lead to volume change and cracking.
