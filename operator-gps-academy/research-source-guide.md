## Research-to-lesson mapping
1. **Stationing and offsets:** alignment origin, direction of increasing station, left/right offset, chosen reference point, display units, cross-section design elevations. Cross-check with L01 dozer/grader videos and L02.
2. **Cut/fill:** design elevation versus measured blade-edge elevation at the **same point**, sign conventions, surface selection and offsets. Cross-check with L01 and L02.
3. **Machine setup:** selected project, approved design revision, coordinate system, calibration, positioning quality and daily independent control checks. L01 all-solutions/earthmoving plus L02.
4. **Troubleshooting:** sudden grade swings, positioning degradation, wrong surface, loose mounts, sensor or machine geometry errors. L02 and approved dealer/site procedures. Never suggest adjusting calibration blindly.

## First example: ready for lesson adaptation
- Scenario: Station 10+250; offset 3.50 m right of alignment in direction of increasing station.
- At same blade-edge point: measured 102.600 m, design 102.450 m; difference +0.150 m above design, i.e. 15 cm of cut **under this lesson's stated convention**.
- Crossfall: centreline 102.520 m, 2% downward toward right, 3.50 m run; 0.070 m fall, so edge design 102.450 m.
- Critical caution: station notation and offset sign/display reference conventions vary by job/system. Verify the alignment, reference point, datum, approved design, machine geometry and sign convention before machine operation.
