Before you trust the number

Confirm the approved design revision, project coordinates, machine and blade setup, positioning quality, offsets, and a physical check against known site control. A correct-looking cut/fill number does not establish that a machine is correctly calibrated.

Never use this training simulator to set a live blade or bucket. Follow manufacturer instructions and site-specific safety procedures; independently verify grade. GNSS guidance does not locate buried utilities.
