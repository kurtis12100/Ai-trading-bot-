## Detailed opening voiceover (recording-ready sample)
“Welcome to Operator GPS Academy. In this lesson, we’re going to make stationing and offsets practical. You’ll see a dozer working beside a road centreline, and we’ll compare what’s on the GPS display with what’s happening in the dirt. Stationing tells you how far along a chosen alignment you are. Offset tells you how far to one side of that alignment a chosen reference point sits. Neither number alone tells you whether your blade is on grade. To answer that, we need the correct design surface, the elevation at the same reference point, and a verified machine setup. Before relying on machine guidance, follow your site’s control and safety procedures. Let’s start with the centreline.”
