# Stationing and Offsets — Priority Instructor Module

**Status:** Script and storyboard, not rendered video. **Runtime target:** 30 minutes within the six-hour curriculum.

## Learning outcomes
Identify station (chainage), signed left/right offset, reference alignment, design elevation, measured blade cutting-edge elevation, and cut/fill sign convention. Never confuse machine-centre position with blade reference position.

## Scene 1 — Establish the reference (0–6 min)
**Visual:** Realistic unbranded dozer beside a road centreline; plan view shows stations 10+200 and 10+250.
**Instructor:** “A station tells you how far along the selected alignment you are. In metric stationing, 10+250 often means 10,250 metres from the alignment origin, but project conventions vary. Check the project definition. Stationing alone does not give your lateral position.”
**Check:** Ask student to identify the station and selected alignment.

## Scene 2 — Left and right offsets (6–12 min)
**Visual:** Camera looking in the direction of increasing station; centreline and offset lines marked at 3.5 m each side.
**Instructor:** “Left and right are determined looking toward increasing station, not necessarily where the dozer is facing. At station 10+250, an offset of 3.5 metres right puts the selected reference point 3.5 metres to the right of the alignment. Confirm which point the display is reporting.”
**Check:** Turn the dozer around; explain why the offset side does not reverse.

## Scene 3 — Read grade at the correct point (12–20 min)
**Visual:** Split screen of side elevation and generic machine-control display: Station 10+250, Offset R 3.50 m, Design 102.450 m, Cutting edge 102.600 m.
**Instructor:** “At the same reference point, the cutting edge is 0.150 metres above design: fifteen centimetres of cut remain, if the project's cut/fill convention defines positive as above design. Check the screen's convention before acting. Never subtract elevations from different points.”
**Visual action:** Blade approaches target in controlled passes; show reading tending toward zero.
**Safety note:** Verify independent site control, correct surface revision, machine calibration, and approved grade checks before relying on guidance.

## Scene 4 — Road crossfall (20–26 min)
**Visual:** Road centreline at 102.520 m, right edge 3.5 m away with a 2% downward crossfall.
**Instructor:** “Two percent over 3.5 metres is a 0.070-metre drop. The design at the right edge is 102.450 metres. The grade changes as the offset changes, even at the same station.”
**Exercise:** At 5 m right offset on the same uniform slope, compute design elevation: 102.420 m.

## Scene 5 — Field challenge and assessment (26–30 min)
1. Identify station 10+250 and offset 3.5 m right.
2. Explain which way is right when the dozer faces decreasing station.
3. Calculate cut/fill from 102.600 m measured and 102.450 m design at the same cutting-edge point.
4. Explain why an incorrect alignment or design revision makes a plausible display unsafe.
5. Stop work and request verification if the display conflicts with independent control or field marks.
